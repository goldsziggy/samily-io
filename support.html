Bedtime Hero

For help with Bedtime Hero — subscriptions, account issues, story generation problems, or refund requests — email samily@zygowicz.com. Please include:

  • The platform you're using (iOS or Android) and your device model.
  • A short description of what happened and what you expected.
  • For subscription issues: the email tied to your App Store or Google Play account (we don't need passwords).

Useful links:

  • BedtimeHero.ai — product website
  • Privacy policy
  • Terms of use
  • App Store: Bedtime Hero
  • Google Play: Bedtime Hero

Subscription & refunds: Subscription billing is handled by Apple (App Store) or Google (Google Play). To cancel or request a refund, manage your subscription in your Apple ID or Google Play account settings. If you need help, we're happy to walk you through it — email us.
