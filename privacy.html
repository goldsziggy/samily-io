Privacy Policy

A plain-English summary of how Samily LLC handles your information.

Last updated:

Who we are

Samily LLC ("Samily," "we," "our") is a small family-run company based in the United States. We make handcrafted goods sold through our Etsy storefront and software products distributed through the Apple App Store and Google Play. You can reach us any time at samily@zygowicz.com.

What this policy covers

This policy describes our practices for this website (samily.io). Each of our apps and our Etsy shop are operated on third-party platforms and have their own privacy disclosures. Where an app collects or processes personal information, the specifics live in that app's own privacy policy, which we link to below.

  • Bedtime Hero — bedtimehero.ai/privacy
  • Familiarium — a privacy policy will be published before launch and linked here.
  • Etsy shop (RaisingArrowstwo) — governed by Etsy's privacy policy. We see only the order details Etsy provides us to fulfill purchases.

Information this website collects

This website is a static marketing site. We do not run analytics, set tracking cookies, or run advertising on samily.io. The only information we receive from this site is:

  • Standard web server logs from our hosting provider (GitHub Pages), which may include IP address, browser user agent, and the page requested. These logs are retained by the hosting provider per their policy and used only for security and abuse prevention.
  • Anything you choose to send us when you email samily@zygowicz.com. We use your message only to respond to you.

How we use information

We use the limited information described above to operate this site, respond to your emails, and protect against abuse. We do not sell personal information, and we do not share it with third parties for marketing.

Children's privacy

This website is not directed to children under 13. Our apps, where used by children, are governed by the privacy policy of that specific app, which describes any age-appropriate handling and parental controls.

Your rights

If you are a resident of a jurisdiction that grants data-subject rights (such as the EU, UK, or California), you may contact us at samily@zygowicz.com to request access to, correction of, or deletion of any personal information we hold about you. We will respond within a reasonable time and at no cost.

Changes

We may update this policy from time to time. The "Last updated" date above will always reflect the most recent revision. Material changes will be highlighted on this page.

Contact

Questions about this policy or our privacy practices: samily@zygowicz.com.
